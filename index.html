FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Questions About Nexus?

Here are some answers to help you understand how the platform works.

How do I join Nexus?

Select Login on the homepage, then choose Sign Up on the login screen to complete your registration.

How can I deposit money?

Sign in to your account and use the deposit section to follow the available payment process.

Can I monitor my transactions?

Yes. Your member dashboard provides access to your available transaction records.

How do I request a withdrawal?

Sign in and submit a withdrawal request through your member dashboard. Requests are subject to the platform's approval process.
