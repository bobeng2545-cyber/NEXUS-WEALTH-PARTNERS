Scheme Authorization Management

Record trustee and scheme authorization evidence and require independent administrator verification.

INDEPENDENT VERIFICATION REQUIRED

Submitting authorization evidence does not mean that a trustee or pension scheme has been legally approved.

The submitting administrator cannot verify their own submission. Two different administrators, neither of whom is the submitter, must independently verify the record.

Regulatory documents and licensing information must be checked against genuine authoritative records.
